01 / Materials

What goes on the roof, and what it costs.

Six systems we install weekly. Every price below includes tear-off of one layer, synthetic underlayment, ice-and-water shield at the eaves and valleys, new drip edge, new pipe boots, and full decking inspection.

01

Architectural asphalt

The default for most KC re-roofs. 30-year manufacturer warranty, hail rated up to Class 4 on certain product lines. Installed with six nails per shingle in the hail belt.

$7.25 per sq ft installed
02

3-tab asphalt

Builder-grade. Cheapest option, shortest life. We install it for rental properties and outbuildings where the budget is fixed and the client understands the trade-off.

$5.25 per sq ft installed
03

Standing-seam metal

24-gauge steel with a Kynar finish, 1-inch mechanical seams, concealed fasteners. Best on low-pitch roofs, modern builds, and commercial buildings where the roof has to last 40-plus years.

$13.50 per sq ft installed
04

Cedar shake

Hand-split western red cedar on 30-pound felt, with a preservative treatment. Suits Brookside, Mission Hills, and other historic neighborhoods where a natural profile belongs.

$11.50 per sq ft installed
05

Synthetic slate

DaVinci or Brava composite tile that reads as slate from the curb, weighs roughly a third as much, and installs at a fraction of the cost of real stone. 50-year limited warranty.

$17.00 per sq ft installed
06

TPO membrane (flat)

For low-slope additions, porch roofs, and flat commercial sections. Mechanically attached, hot-air welded seams, 20-year warranty on the field membrane.

$9.50 per sq ft installed

04 / Warranty

Three tiers. All transferable.

Every Ironclad install carries a manufacturer warranty on the material and an Ironclad workmanship warranty on the labor. The workmanship warranty transfers to the next owner if you sell within the coverage term.

Tier 01

10-year workmanship

Standard with any 3-tab or non-system asphalt install. Covers leaks caused by installation error, not storm damage.

  • 10 years, Ironclad labor warranty
  • Manufacturer warranty on shingles
  • Transferable once, at no cost
  • Annual inspection included for the first three years

Tier 02 · Most common

25-year system

With a full GAF or Owens Corning system install: shingles, underlayment, starter strip, ridge cap, and ventilation all from one manufacturer.

  • 25 years, Ironclad labor warranty
  • Manufacturer system warranty (up to 50 years on material)
  • Transferable once within the first 20 years
  • Annual inspection included for the first ten years

Tier 03

30-year restoration

Premium systems: DaVinci composite slate, standing-seam metal, and natural slate restoration with sourced salvage.

  • 30 years, Ironclad labor warranty
  • Manufacturer warranty per material (up to 50 years)
  • Fully transferable to next owner
  • Annual inspection for the life of the workmanship warranty

05 / Process

Four steps. No surprises.

Every Ironclad job runs through the same sequence. The inspection is free, the quote is fixed, the crew is ours, and the final walk-through happens with the foreman who ran the job.

01

Inspection

A foreman walks the roof, photographs every slope, and checks the deck, flashing, and ventilation. Takes about 45 minutes.

02

Fixed quote

You get a written scope with material specs, timeline, payment schedule, and a fixed price. No change orders unless you change the scope.

03

Tear-off & install

Two to four days on most homes. Decking inspected and repaired before underlayment goes down. Dumpster on-site, yard swept nightly.

04

Final walk-through

Foreman on the ground with you, camera in hand. Magnet sweep of the yard, warranty documents filed, inspection report emailed.
